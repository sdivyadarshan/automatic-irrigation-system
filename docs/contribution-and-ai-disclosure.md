# Contribution and AI Disclosure

This document separates the student's physical project work from AI-assisted support. It does not claim that AI performed physical assembly or testing.

## Student contribution

Based on the project development discussion, the student:
- Selected and assembled the Arduino Nano-based irrigation prototype.
- Worked with the soil-moisture sensor module, relay, 5 V pump, buzzer, breadboard, and jumper wires.
- Made and revised physical wiring connections.
- Uploaded sketches and performed hands-on tests.
- Observed relay, pump, buzzer behavior, and unstable sensor readings.
- Reported test outcomes and specified the two-mode behavior and audible transition indication.

## AI-assisted contribution

AI assistance was used to:
- Explain component pin functions, Nano power pins, relay contacts, and wiring.
- Draft and revise Arduino code for sensing, relay control, buzzer alerts, averaging, and hysteresis.
- Suggest a debugging sequence for isolating sensor, relay, buzzer, and pump issues.
- Prepare repository structure, README, algorithm, architecture, design-decision, and testing documentation.

## Verification responsibility

The student should verify wiring against actual module labels and datasheets, calibrate thresholds using the physical sensor and target soil, test relay polarity and pump supply current, understand the source code, and update testing records with measured results.

AI assistance is disclosed for transparency. It does not replace the student's hardware assembly, observations, or responsibility for validating the final prototype.
